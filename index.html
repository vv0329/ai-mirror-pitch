The offer

What it is

A mirror that fits retail — with the full deployment behind it.

It is a touchscreen mirror with a built-in computer. We design the interface, install it on site, set up the try-on, and keep the software running.

  • Customers can browse products on the touchscreen.
  • They can take a photo and preview an item on their body.
  • The mirror can show preset outfit suggestions.
  • Staff can use the same screen to look up basic product info.
What it is not

It does not replace staff. It is not a plug-and-play box you switch on and forget. We sell a deployment service, not just a screen rental.

Honest edges

Risks & limitations

We’ll tell you when not to pitch it.

Preview, not fabric truth

On-body preview is a generated image — not precise pattern simulation. Complex fabrics and special cuts may not render well. We assess product type before leaning on try-on.

Modules need readiness

Inventory and checkout need an API. Idle playback needs your films. If it can’t integrate, we say so upfront.

You bring product truth

Images, info and outfit presets need basic assets from your side. We configure; content accuracy stays with you.

People still matter

Staff briefing frames the mirror as workload relief — not surveillance or replacement. Without that, deployment fails.

No pop-up / event need and no interest in occasional sale-saving? Don’t rent it. Idle hardware hurts everyone.
